# 视觉叙事 · Visual Storytelling

This migrated Skill preserves the existing visual constitution: one emotion, one story, one moment, one hook; vivid color with low content density; breathing room; painterly material; and no text, logos, seals, signatures or watermarks in the artwork layer.

It emits two views of one Narrative Core: the image prompt is the moment worth stopping on, and the video storyboard is the restrained movement that leads into and out of it. Painting language, healing mood, composition, material and anti-drift rules remain plugin-owned.
