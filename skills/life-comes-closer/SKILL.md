# 自然靠近你 · Life Comes Closer

This plugin owns the visual intelligence for a quiet encounter: a life form notices a person, moves closer, and leaves a soft afterglow. It always returns the Dual Output Contract.

The shared Narrative Core is the source for both outputs: the image prompt holds the decisive moment, while the video storyboard shows how the encounter approaches, happens, and settles. Camera placement, natural texture, emotional timing and scene language remain inside this Skill.
