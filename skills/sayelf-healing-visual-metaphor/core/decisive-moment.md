# Decisive Moment

## TURN → MOMENT → AFTERGLOW

```text
TURN       the one small change in behavior or relationship
MOMENT     the exact visual instant that captures that change
AFTERGLOW  what remains true after the change, without closure
```

Prefer the instant just before, during, or just after a change, not a completed victory:

```text
hand opening, not object already gone
foot reaching the edge, not a bridge already built
flower first noticed, not a garden in full bloom
rain still falling, but arms opening
```

## Hero frame rule

The Hero Image must freeze the `MOMENT` at the highest meaning density. It is not automatically Shot 01 or a random video frame. State what is suspended in the air, held at the edge, newly visible, or just beginning.

## Afterglow rule

The final state may be lighter, clearer, or more open, but not complete. Keep one unresolved truth from the `WOUND` or obstacle. Silence is part of the ending.

## Candidate test

Accept a `MOMENT` only if:

1. it can be drawn as one frame;
2. it changes the character/world relationship;
3. it needs no caption to carry the idea;
4. it leaves room for an `AFTERGLOW`.
