# Rule: Visual Contrast

Use a deliberate contrast between a credible world and a simplified emotional proxy when that contrast clarifies the metaphor:

```text
real world × minimal avatar
dark field × one meaningful light
large environment × small decisive gesture
```

Keep the contrast stable across the Hero Image and all five shots. Contrast is structural; it is not permission for excessive glow, fantasy particles, or unrelated styles.

## Action and color readability

Treat legibility as a generation priority, not a finishing adjustment:

```text
clear silhouette + separated joints + visible contact point
+ pure signature hues + strong value separation
```

Describe the action before decorative atmosphere. Keep the full pose large enough
to read, leave clean negative space around the arms and legs, and name the limb
sequence explicitly when it matters: upper arm → forearm → palm and thigh → shin
→ foot. Use saturated, distinguishable signature colors against a deep or otherwise
clearly separated value field. Bloom, haze, reflections, and gradients must not
merge the limbs, flatten the silhouette, or make adjacent colors ambiguous.
