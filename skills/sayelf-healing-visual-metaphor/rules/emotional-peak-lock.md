# Frozen Rule: EMOTIONAL PEAK LOCK

The five shots contain one dominant emotional peak, located at `Shot 04 · TURN`.

```text
WORLD      quiet baseline
WOUND      pressure becomes readable
TENSION    pressure concentrates
TURN       one peak and one change
AFTERGLOW  release of intensity, not a second event
```

Do not add a second reveal, surprise, transformation, music swell, or visual effect after the `TURN`. The last shot holds the consequence and lets the viewer complete the meaning.
