# Rule: Lighting

Choose one dominant light logic and keep its direction stable:

- acceptance/rain: cool dark environment with one restrained warm distant light;
- hope/distance: deep navy field with cool avatar light and one warm destination light;
- release/persistence: natural directional light that reveals the next surface without making the ending bright and complete.

Light should reveal the relationship and the `MOMENT`, not turn the frame into a generic inspirational poster.
