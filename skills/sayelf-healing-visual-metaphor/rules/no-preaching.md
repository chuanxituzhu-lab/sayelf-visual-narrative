# Frozen Rule: NO PREACHING

Never write the lesson into the scene. Do not add moral captions, therapeutic promises, diagnosis, direct advice, a character delivering a speech, or a forced happy ending.

Show only:

```text
one person or proxy
one world
one relationship
one small change
one unfinished final image
```

If the concept cannot be read without explanatory text, simplify the relationship or sharpen the `MOMENT`; do not add more copy.
