# Example 02: 悬崖与花 / 希望

Regression source: the local cliff-and-flower reference from the current conversation. The supplied image is a vertical social screenshot with an overlaid “Your Plan” label, arrow, controls, counters, caption, and account UI; those are treated as source noise and removed from the visual package.

## Input

> 看得见希望，不等于已经有路；但你仍然可以先走到边缘。

## Reference observations

```text
Observation: two rocky cliff masses face each other across a deep vertical void;
            a tiny cool blue-white line figure is on the left;
            one small warm golden flower is on the right;
            the scene is deep navy at night with a crescent moon and distant landscape.
Inference:  the destination is visible while the route remains absent; the figure is anonymous.
Hypothesis: the smallest change is not crossing the abyss but taking one step toward the edge.
```
